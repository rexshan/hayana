1. Data we process and why

Account and sign-in
We use your mobile number, including its country calling code, and the verification information you provide to verify phone sign-in and link it to your haya account. A mobile number is required for phone sign-in. If you sign in with your HAYA ID and password, we use those details to authenticate you.
Profile
We use the details you enter, such as your display name and any country you select when prompted, to create and update your profile. If you choose an avatar from your device, we receive the image you select for upload and use it to display your profile photo. Adding a photo is optional.
Direct messages and safety reports
When you send a text message, we process the message and the account information needed to deliver it to the intended recipient and display the conversation. If you block a user, we use that selection to restrict contact. If you report a user or content, we use the details you provide to investigate and respond.
Breathing tools
haya uses your selected practice settings to run guided breathing exercises and uses session details to show your practice history.
Language settings
haya uses the language you select to display the app in that language.
Connection information
Your device uses an internet connection for online features. The service uses the IP address associated with requests to establish the connection and return responses.
Support and privacy requests
If you contact us, we receive the contact details and information you provide, including attachments you choose to send. We use them to respond, verify requests and resolve the issue. Providing relevant information helps us locate the account or records involved.

We process the data needed to deliver requested features and perform our agreement with you. Where permitted by applicable law, we rely on our legitimate interests in securing accounts and preventing abuse. We also process data when needed to comply with a legal obligation. If a use requires consent, we ask for it separately.

Confirm which profile fields other users can see and which practice history information stays on the device or syncs to the service.

2. Android device access

Internet access
haya uses the internet to sign you in, deliver messages, upload your chosen profile photo and process account requests.
Photos and media
When you choose a profile image, you can select a photo from your device for upload. You can decline to select a photo or change any media permission you have granted in Android settings. A photo from your device can be uploaded only if you select one.

Check the Android release against this section. Use internet access for online features and a system picker for a selected profile photo. Confirm whether the build checks network connection status and how it handles access to the selected photo.

3. Sharing and cross-border processing

  • Other haya users. Recipients can see the messages you send to them and the profile details shown with the conversation, such as your display name and avatar.
  • SMS verification provider. When you request an SMS sign-in code, we share your mobile phone number, including its country calling code, with Guangzhou Junshu Technology Co., Ltd. to deliver the one-time code.
  • Email service. Our email provider transmits and stores the correspondence you send to the listed mailbox so that we can respond to support and privacy requests.
  • Competent authorities. We may disclose the information required to meet a legal obligation, comply with a valid legal request or protect an individual's safety where applicable law permits.

This version of haya has no integrated third-party SDKs. We are a Hong Kong company. Where personal data is transferred or accessed across borders, we limit the transfer to the information needed and apply the safeguards required by applicable law, including Saudi Arabia's Personal Data Protection Law where it applies.

Before publication, check the SMS provider's English company name against its contract. Identify the countries or regions where data is stored or processed, including for backups and SMS verification. Confirm whether personal data is handled outside Saudi Arabia, the applicable safeguards and any additional service providers.

4. Retention and security

Account and profile data
We keep your phone number, account identifier and current profile while your account is open so you can sign in and use your profile.
Messages and practice information
We keep service-held messages for delivery and for as long as needed to provide conversation history in haya. Service-held practice information is kept for as long as needed to provide the history or preferences you use.
Support, privacy and safety records
We keep the information needed to resolve a request or report and to document the outcome where required by law.

We delete personal data when it is no longer needed for the stated purpose, except where a legal obligation or an active legal proceeding requires continued retention. In those cases, access and use are limited to that purpose, and the information is deleted when the reason to retain it ends.

We use technical and organisational safeguards to limit access to personal data and protect it from accidental loss, unauthorised access and disclosure.

Confirm the retention period or criteria for each category, including any diagnostic or security logs. Confirm the safeguards and any legal retention periods in use.

5. Account deletion

In the app. Go to Me → Settings → Account Management → Delete Account. Read the confirmation and select Delete Account to submit your request.

Outside the app. Email pertamais1st@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my haya account”. We will ask for the information needed to locate the account and verify that the request is yours. Keep your password and verification codes private.

We will delete your account and its associated personal data within 15 calendar days. For an in-app request, the period starts when you submit it. For an emailed request, it starts when we receive the information needed to verify that the account is yours.

Deletion is permanent. It covers your phone number, account identifier, profile, avatar, and any messages and breathing practice records associated with your account that we hold. Where a service provider holds account data for us, we will have the relevant data deleted as required. Copies that a recipient has saved outside haya are held by the recipient. We retain specific records only when legally required, and remove them when the requirement ends.

Verify that account deletion reaches account records, messages, profile images, practice history and retained copies, including backups, within the stated period unless specific records must be retained by law.

6. Your privacy rights

You can contact us to ask how we process your personal data, access it or request a readable copy, and ask us to correct, complete, update or delete it. Where applicable law provides this right, you can ask us to restrict processing while we check disputed information. If we rely on your consent for a particular use, you can withdraw that consent.

Send rights requests to pertamais1st@gmail.com. We may take reasonable steps to verify your identity. See section 5 for account deletion requests. For other rights requests under Saudi Arabia's Personal Data Protection Law, we will act without undue delay and within 30 days of receiving the request. Where an extension is permitted, we will tell you why before extending the period by up to 30 more days.

You may lodge a complaint with the competent data protection authority. In Saudi Arabia this is the Saudi Data & AI Authority (SDAIA) where its law applies.

7. Age eligibility

haya is for users aged 18 or older, measured by the Gregorian calendar, who meet the eligibility requirements in the Terms of Service. A parent or guardian who believes that someone under 18 has provided personal data to haya can contact us. We will investigate and take appropriate measures, including deleting data where required by law.

8. Changes to this Policy
