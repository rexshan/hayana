Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 10, 2026

Haya is a breathing practice and calendar app. This policy explains how the Android app handles your information.

Haya is provided by pertama 1st (“we,” “us,” or “our”). You can use Haya without creating an account.

1. Information stored on your device

Haya stores the following information in the app’s local storage to provide its features:

  • Practice records: a session identifier, the session’s start time, breathing pattern, completed cycles, and duration. These records let you review your practice history and statistics.
  • Preferences: sound, vibration, keeping the screen awake during practice, and your selected cycle goal.

The app does not send these records or preferences to us. It does not include advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting services. It does not access your location, contacts, camera, microphone, or Android Health Connect data.

2. Android backup and device transfer

Haya allows Android’s system backup and restore features. Depending on your device, Android version, and settings, the operating system may back up app data to your chosen backup service or transfer it to another device. This may include your practice records and preferences.

These features are managed by Android and your backup provider. We do not operate a backup server or receive access to those backups. You can manage backup and restore options through your device and backup account settings.

3. When you contact us

If you email us, we receive your email address and any information you choose to include. We use this information to respond to your inquiry and provide support. Please avoid sending sensitive information that is unnecessary for your request.

Our email provider processes these messages to deliver and store email. Support messages are retained only for as long as needed to resolve your inquiry and any related follow-up, unless a longer retention period is legally required.

4. Sharing information

We do not sell your personal information or use it for advertising. The app does not transmit your practice records or preferences to us or to third-party services. Android-managed backups and information you voluntarily send by email are handled as described above.

5. Keeping and deleting your data

Your practice records remain in local app storage until you delete them. You can delete individual records or clear your practice history within Haya. To remove all local records and preferences, clear Haya’s app storage in Android settings. Uninstalling the app normally also removes its local data.

Deleting local data does not necessarily delete an existing system backup, and a restore may bring back previously backed-up data. Manage or delete backups through your device or backup provider’s settings.

To request deletion of information you sent by email, contact us using the address below. We cannot remotely access or delete practice records stored only on your device.

6. Protecting information

Haya uses Android’s app-private storage for practice records and preferences. Device security and the protection of system backups also depend on your device settings and backup provider. We limit use of support messages to handling your inquiry.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when Haya’s features or data practices change. The updated policy will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
